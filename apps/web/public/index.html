How it works

One conversation. Four clear steps.

The whole transfer happens inside WhatsApp. You state what you want, approve the exact numbers, then Seti handles the rest.

1

Message your request

Send a plain-language message like "Send KES 3,500 to 0722111222." Seti parses the recipient, amount, and currency.

2

Review the quote

The backend computes the rate, fee, and total, then locks the quote for 45 seconds. You see exactly what leaves your wallet and what the recipient gets.

3

Confirm with the quote ID and nonce

Reply with the full confirmation string. A single confirmation executes once. Replaying it returns the cached result, not a new charge.

4

Track until payout

Seti swaps your stablecoin, settles on-chain, then releases fiat through Pretium. You only see completed once the mobile money network confirms the payout.

Safety

Built so a failed step never becomes a lost step.

Moving money across messaging, blockchain, and mobile money means things can go wrong. Seti resolves ambiguity instead of hiding it.

Idempotent by design

One confirmation executes exactly once. Retry the same quote and you get the cached result, not a second charge.

Balanced ledger

We record every movement as a double-entry journal, enforce it at the database level, and group it per asset.

Independent verification

Seti confirms every settlement transfer on-chain before telling Pretium about it. It polls payout status instead of relying only on webhooks.

Automatic refunds

If a transfer fails after funds move on-chain but before fiat payout completes, we reverse the position and refund your wallet without you asking.

Human escalation

If a transaction cannot be automatically resolved within SLA, a human reviewer takes it over. Nothing is left stuck.

Quote preview

You approve the exact numbers.

No hidden re-pricing. No rounding. The quote shows the rate, fees, and total before you confirm.
